## Platform

web

## Users
Finnish homeowners considering painting or wallpapering work for their home. They browse on desktop or phone, often while planning a renovation, and want to quickly judge whether this local company is competent and reachable. *(Inferred from site content and language; user directed the agent to proceed on these assumptions.)*

## Product Purpose
A single-page presentation site for a local painting and wallpapering company. Its job: make the company look competent and make contacting for a quote one action away. Success = the visitor calls or emails.

## Positioning
A local, hands-on tradesperson: real work, clean results, direct contact — no middlemen, no booking system. *(Inferred.)*

## Operating Context
Static site, Finnish language. Four sections: etusivu (hero), palvelut (services), meistä (about), yhteystiedot (contact).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS only; no framework, no build step. *(Inferred from project structure: school final project under "JavaScriptHarjoitukset".)*
- Contact details are placeholders (+358 00 000 0000, info@kotkantienmaalaus.fi) and must not be presented as verified. *(Placeholder status inferred; user did not confirm.)*
- Language: Finnish.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Kotkantien Maalaus ja Tapetointi" (pinned by user).
- Language: Finnish (pinned by user).
- Four-section structure: etusivu, palvelut, meistä, yhteystiedot (pinned by user).

## Evidence on Hand
No photos, no real testimonials, no real contact info, no logo asset. Future work must not fabricate customers, prices, or reviews; any illustrative content must be labeled as such.

## Product Principles
1. Trust over decoration: the site must read as a competent tradesperson, not a template.
2. One action: everything funnels to "ota yhteyttä" (contact for a quote).
3. Finnish-first: all copy in Finnish, plain and direct.
4. Lightweight: static, fast, no dependencies.
